The asset is a local MP4 copy downloaded from the Pexels video CDN and is not fetched externally at runtime. Verify the exact downloaded clip and rights before redistributing it outside this project. Pexels license terms apply; identify yourself as the downloader/project owner, and do not imply endorsement by the depicted people or company.

Images were downloaded from their original Unsplash image CDN, resized preserving aspect ratio, encoded as WebP at quality0.76, stripped of unnecessary metadata through canvas export, and provided in two responsive sizes. Exact dimensions and byte sizes are in Front-End/public/images/logistics-images.json. Descriptive alt text is on meaningful images; the login background also has a descriptive stock-photo alternative. No faces/brands are used to claim endorsement. The previous original JPEG files are retained for compatibility but are no longer used by current screens; their original license provenance was not available and they should not be republished independently without review.
